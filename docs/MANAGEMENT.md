# Hotel management - version 9.0

Open **MANAGE** from the hotel HUD. The board keeps the hotel running and shows emergency/police alerts. Regular conversations pause gameplay. Existing checkpoints migrate when the hotel opens; save at the desk to retain progress.

## Time, guests and contracts
The day starts at 08:00. Normal arrivals stop at 20:00; night coverage is more expensive and problems become more frequent. One game hour takes roughly twenty running seconds. Books offers an advance-hour button. The end of the night rolls into the next morning. Rest is available at night after police cases are settled.

Desk sets budget, standard, surge or premium prices and nightly, hourly or weekly contracts. Hourly bookings last two game hours; weekly bookings last seven nights with a 20% discount. Guest budgets, reputation, amenities and demand affect rates and successful assignments. Private cash guests and adult hourly guests need identity verification. Optional shady mode permits unverified/night rentals and adds police attention.

Reservations arrive later or generate a disclosed ten-coin no-show fee. Normal travelers, party guests, private cash travelers, long-term residents, adult sex workers, suspicious dealmakers, cult visitors and hidden celebrities have distinct needs and budgets. The recurring cast keeps its identity and appearance. Personal service builds trust, which changes willingness to pay.

## Rooms and supplies
Rooms have basic, deluxe, suite and hourly types; cleanliness, condition, damage and infestation state persist. Dirty or faulty rooms cannot be assigned. Suites require reputation 60. Cleaning and repair mini-games use a green timing window; three successful actions complete the task if supplies are available.

Cleaning uses detergent and towels. Check-in uses toiletries and towels; hourly bookings also draw hygiene supplies when available. Repairs consume parts and coins; pest treatment consumes pesticide. Room upgrades provide actual effects:
- Better bed: higher rates and better comfort reviews.
- Soundproofing: prevents party/noise penalties.
- Mini-fridge: consumes snacks for extra occupied-room income.
- Strong locks: protect against vandalism damage.
- Door cameras: provide extra time to respond to incidents.

## People and events
Hire housekeepers, maintenance, security, a car-wash attendant and Nora the front-desk clerk. Speed affects work cadence; skill affects emergency response; honesty affects missing cash. Training costs 25 and improves skills, loyalty and fatigue. Add night coverage for 50% extra wages. Exhaustion slows work and can cause drama; unpaid or unhappy staff can quit.

Events covers noise, vandalism, bedbugs, roaches, power outages, water leaks, AC failures, Wi-Fi, privacy and temperature complaints, plus unusual visitors and requests. Staff can handle suitable tasks when on duty and stocked. Ignored problems damage reviews, relationships, condition and bills. Sufficient police attention summons a business citation independently of fatal emergencies.

## Costs, growth and monitoring
Rent and utilities are charged each morning alongside payroll. Unpaid bills become debt; emergency credit provides 200 with 240 due and a capped outstanding balance. Books records bookings, supplies, wages, bills, services, repairs and debts.

The initial lot supports six rooms. Adjacent land opens nine; an upper floor opens eighteen, including rooms 201-209. To use upstairs doors, stand outside and select the upper floor in Growth. Other developments include a staffed pool, patio restaurant, six-bay parking, fiber Wi-Fi, CCTV and night vision.

Cameras monitors the grounds and corridors; interiors remain private. Power failures disable the camera network. Night vision is available after buying the network and night-vision equipment.

Festivals raise demand and noise; tourist season supports better rooms; factory closures bring weekly residents; heatwaves stress air conditioning. Reviews and reputation affect future demand and unlock development.

## Story
Ethical, greedy, criminal and cult-friendly decisions lead to different outcomes; serious debt can produce foreclosure. Endings can be recorded after day 14 or eighteen rooms, and play can continue. Shady offers are disabled by default and are fictional game choices. Existing police fines/bribes, car washing, weather, family reception, stealth and checkpoint recovery remain available.

Employees can approach a job from the side if a guest or the player occupies its exact destination. Maintenance and security travel to their assignments before working. Guest identity and facial reactions remain intact during emergencies.
