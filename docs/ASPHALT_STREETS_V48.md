# Asphalt streets

The road material selector (kind 2) now draws a continuous cool-gray asphalt surface with faint subpixel grain instead of the pebble-path bitmap. Pedestrian paving retains its smaller warm stones. Road tile selection and all navigation/collision geometry are unchanged.

The existing horizontal road keeps its dashed yellow center markings. The north/south street and the eastern road gain matching markings, interrupted at their existing junctions. No curbs or solid props have been added. Native preview mode --ui-street captures the intersection beside Coral Catch and the beach entrance from the user's screenshot.

The v47 water animation, original characters, controls, save schema and v45 startup fix are preserved. The original road asset remains in the catalog and source sheets as a backup; it is no longer selected by the live outdoor ground renderer.
